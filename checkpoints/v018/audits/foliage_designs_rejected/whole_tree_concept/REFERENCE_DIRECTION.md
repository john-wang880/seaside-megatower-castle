## General form principles
Tapered whole crown; radial outward main boughs with modest ascending tips; secondary foliage hangs downward; asymmetric separated tiers toward the outside and overlapping opaque core. Adapt principles to compact stylized thick closed wedge/fan masses, not fine individual needles, thin ladders or stacked vertical pears. Retain considerably denser mass than the botanical silhouette rather than repeating sparse trial failure.

Preserve overall crown height/volume/footprint, original trunk/roots/context and palette, route clearance, and recoverable original forest. Foliage may be reorganized across legacy clump boundaries. Do not confuse per-clump identity with a user constraint. First write coherent design and create cheap isolated geometry renders front/side/oblique with silhouette diagnostics; no full-scene integration/render until concept passes. A visually weak concept stops rather than becoming another parameter loop or model version.
