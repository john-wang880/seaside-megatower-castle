## Important source constraint, not a defect diagnosis

The exact fixed lip is strongly irregular: 3D length1,263.863 versus XY543.632; Z spans −1.160…48.845. Column134 is Z0.372 between high neighbors ~29/31; several152–161 columns are Z6.84 beside ~43. These source depressions affect all descending rings, not just an invented sample. Fixed lip preservation necessarily retains deep channels/zigzags. A globally smooth cliff top is therefore **not** deliverable under this initial constraint. The proposal can broaden lower profiles while retaining them, but whole-view art review must decide whether this is coherent. If not, request an explicitly mapped upper-rim revision outside support/route cores; do not force fins or quietly flatten the source.

## Occupied support policy and gates

Proposed policy is **no excavation of old occupied material**: the eventual connected solid must be proven a superset of the current western source while retaining all exact support cores. The offset field alone does not prove this, especially between irregular sections. Use independent old/new occupied-set and boundary tests; if local union is required, it must preserve fixed seams and avoid duplicate exposed skins. Do not report that no-source-volume loss already holds.

Current 3D coast/architecture disjointness and protected XY columns235–242 do not validate this larger domain. Before prototype, test full proposed surface/occupied volume against actual exported architectural/approach/path triangles, retained root seats, and the separately protected marker area. Preserve whole marker rock and its four direct-scene architectural components; the latter lack a refreshed complete contact export, so any possible envelope approach triggers a supplemental proof rather than assumed clearance. Preserve main pedestal/blades pending their supporting/decorative partition. No numeric minimum support clearance is claimed here.

Upper and endpoint boundaries must match exact native vertices/edges, bottom closure must remain exact; same-domain splitting must not introduce cracks or coincident visible skins. Full nonlocal triangle intersections, winding, water component changes, old-volume containment and new support-domain exclusions remain prerequisites. A first whole-view B0 comparison should combine coast and grove under original lights; no closeup or camera search to rescue weak form.
